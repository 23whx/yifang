# Mahjong implementation boundary

## Architecture

Rules and game state stay in browsers. No application backend, accounts, database or persistent money/chip assets. A future room host is one player's browser, not a server. Course scripts remain separate from the free-play engine.

## Implemented foundations

- MCR settlement foundation (2026-09-14): `mcr-settlement.ts` consumes a host-evaluated non-flower score, checks the eight-point minimum before adding unique exposed flowers, calculates three baseline payments plus the discard/self-draw hand-value payments, and posts each hand exactly once into a detached zero-sum points ledger. Draws settle zero and also consume the hand identity. `verify-mcr-settlement.cjs` passed 1,008 payment contexts and explicit examples: 8 non-flower + 2 flower points gives +34 on discard or +54 on self draw, not a flower-assisted qualification. Invalid/duplicate flowers, source seats, replay and numeric overflow are rejected; type/lint passed. Reference: EMA Green Book settlement formula and flower exclusion. **Input is not a scoring oracle or guest payload**: fan detection/exclusions, valid hand/event proof and arbitration remain to implement before this may be used by free play.

- MCR setup foundation (2026-09-14): `mcr-setup.ts` validates a 144-tile wall (four copies of each normal kind plus eight unique flowers/seasons), deals four seats, and exposes one explicit flower-replacement action at a time from the wall tail. Replacement chains remain manual; views hide opponents' hands and private replacement tiles. `verify-mcr-setup.cjs` passed 400 setups/all dealer positions and 1,025 replacements, plus a forced chain, inventory/immutability/privacy/stale-input checks. TypeScript and targeted lint passed. Reference: [EMA MCR Green Book section 3.5.6](https://mahjong-europe.org/portal/images/docs/mcr_EN.pdf), text retrieved through search indexing after direct PDF timeout. This module is NOT connected to UI or a full MCR round/scorer; fan exclusions, eight-point qualification, claims, in-play flowers, scoring and networking remain outstanding. It assumes an already shuffled/broken wall, not a physical dice animation.

- `sichuan-round.ts` now connects ready setup to explicit draw/discard, immutable claim windows, pung, three kong forms, replacement draws, rob-kong rollback, winner retirement, result acknowledgement and terminal wall/three-winner states. Emits events but deliberately no fabricated score. `verify-sichuan-round.cjs`: 100 wall-valid seeded rounds, 12,174 actions, 17 win pauses, 99 kong events; inventory/revision/immutability checks at each transition plus a valid opening-win fixture. Rare rob-kong and multiple-winner retirement need directed fixtures. The acknowledgement API is a host/session command; network integration must collect all required consents before invoking it. No Sichuan UI/scorer/room is connected yet. Older “setup only” statements below describe those individual modules, not the whole current codebase.

- `sichuan-claims.ts`: immutable host-derived response windows for discarded tiles and robbing an added kong. All eligible seats respond before resolution; multiple wins override meld claims independent of packet arrival order. Non-winning responses to a legal win are returned for pass-state tracking; only a seat's own options/reply are projected. `verify-sichuan-claims.cjs` covers six arrival permutations, priority, duplicate rejection, privacy and rob-kong filtering. Synthetic arbitration fixtures do not claim a valid full wall. Round integration, kong rollback/commit and payments remain pending.

- `sichuan-actions.ts`: host-side action eligibility and immutable discard, separate from Japanese rules. Covers forced missing-suit discard, structural win candidates, passed-discard-win restriction (not self draw), pung, concealed/added/discard kong, no replacement without wall tiles, and retired seats. Preset currently requires clearing the missing suit before calls and timely added kong using the just-drawn tile. These choices must be documented in the eventual table UI. `verify-sichuan-actions.cjs` passes covered cases; it is not claim arbitration, round progression or scoring.

- `sichuan-setup.ts`: independent 108-tile four-seat setup, fixed pre-deal exchange direction, optional simultaneous same-suit exchange-three, simultaneous missing-suit commitment, immutable input and per-seat allowlisted view. All 12 dealer/direction combinations are tested for inventory, delivery and privacy in `verify-sichuan-setup.cjs`. This is setup ONLY: drawing, claims, retiring winners, scoring, end-of-wall settlement and its UI/network connection remain to implement. It must not be advertised as a playable Sichuan game yet.

- `lib/mahjong/shape.ts`: tile inventory and meld validation; normal decompositions, seven pairs with regional quad distinction, thirteen orphans for Japanese/MCR, MCR knitted shapes, Sichuan missing-suit restrictions. Structural waits are not legal-win decisions.
- `lib/mahjong/riichi-score.ts`: Japanese scoring adapter. Caller supplies the concealed hand **before** the winning tile, melds, winning tile and event context. Tile kinds follow the site's atlas (31 red, 32 green, 33 white), not the scoring library's dragon order.
- Scoring uses `@kobalab/majiang-core` exactly 1.4.1, MIT, https://github.com/kobalab/majiang-core. License distributed at `/licenses/majiang-core.txt`.
- `lib/mahjong/riichi-player.ts` connects a seat's legal decisions to the library's full round/match progression. It offers discard, riichi, chi/pon/kan, ron/tsumo, nine-terminals abort and explicit result acknowledgement when legal. Replies use a per-seat revision and a private allowlist; stale, duplicate and forged choices are rejected. Final event output excludes the full private-hand replay. Shared `riichiRules()` keeps standalone scoring and round settings consistent.
- `lib/mahjong/riichi-session.ts` provides a cancellable local East-round session, one human and three computer opponents. The practice policy prioritizes a legal win/riichi, otherwise minimizes shanten using only its own hand; it declines optional calls and is not advertised as strong AI. Human result acknowledgement gates progression. Stopping closes all player inputs, cancels tracked bot/delayed-transition timers and suppresses already-queued game progression. No backend, transport or persistence is used.

## Japanese scoring preset

No red fives; open tanyao allowed; double yakuman and counted yakuman disabled; multiple distinct yakuman allowed; no rounding up to mangan. Other scoring behavior currently inherits the pinned library's defaults. This is an application preset, not a claim to implement every WRC tournament rule. Before releasing free play, document its full table rules in beginner-facing language.

Score deltas are ordered East, South, West, North. Deposited riichi sticks are awarded from the existing pot: their sum may be positive by that pot amount. The future round engine must deduct deposits once when declared, then clear the awarded pot once; it must not treat these deltas as independently zero-sum.

Ron checks own discards against the entire structural wait set, plus temporary/riichi furiten flags. Furiten does not prohibit self draw. The adapter does not derive flags from turn history. It must not accept player-supplied event flags as authoritative in room messages.

## Not implemented yet

- The library now supplies wall/dead-wall lifecycle, claim priority, furiten transitions, riichi deposits, draws, continuation and match settlement through the tested player adapter and local lifecycle wrapper. These paths still need more targeted scenario tests beyond the seeded matches, beginner UI, network-disconnect handling and actual browser acceptance. Do not describe an unconnected adapter as a finished free-play feature.
- Full independent MCR fan aggregation/exclusions/minimum eligibility and Sichuan scoring/retirement/end-of-wall accounting.
- Four-seat Japanese network integration is implemented via three native manual WebRTC channels, with no signaling/STUN/TURN service. The recorded in-app four-tab acceptance completed an exhaustive draw, four-way acknowledgement, East 2 seat rotation and disconnect freeze in Chinese/English (see completion-audit.md). This is same-computer evidence, not physical LAN, cross-network or phone/touch proof. Network win-result reveal and rare-rule scenarios remain unverified. Local free-play is also connected. All pinned yaku/draw labels have bilingual explanations; exhaustive pedagogy remains pending.

## Evidence

Run `node scripts/verify-mahjong-shape.cjs` and `node scripts/verify-riichi-score.cjs`. The former includes an independent standard decomposition cross-check; the latter uses specified expected payments and eligibility cases, not a second invocation of the same scorer as its oracle. Passing them proves only covered foundation behavior, not a playable or browser-verified full game.

`node scripts/verify-riichi-player.cjs` runs eight seeded full East-round matches through the actual library Game and four adapter players: 41 hands, 28 wins, 13 draws, 259 chosen calls and 3156 decisions. Final points total 100,000 and ranks form 1–4. It checks stale/forged/duplicate input rejection, detached choice arrays, seat-only starting hands and redacted final output. The test policy uses its own hand's shanten, not hidden opponents or the wall. This is a headless integration test, not proof of every rare rule or network/mobile usability.

Integration reference: https://github.com/kobalab/majiang-core/wiki/Majiang.Game and https://github.com/kobalab/majiang-core/wiki/Majiang.Player, cross-checked against installed version 1.4.1 source.

`node scripts/verify-riichi-session.cjs` uses a fake scheduler with the real asynchronous Game path. It verifies cancel before initial dispatch, cancel while awaiting human input, duplicate start/submission rejection, result retention until explicit acknowledgement, a six-hand match with three computer opponents, rank validity and total-point conservation. This is not a browser rendering or real-timer performance test.
